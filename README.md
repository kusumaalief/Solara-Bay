Solara Bay v2 is a 3D blocky open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built with Three.js (vendored locally — the game makes no network requests). You play Leo Marin, back in Solara Bay and in debt: explore a 640×480 blocky city on foot or behind the wheel, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, and run five story missions — Borrowed Wheels, The Courier, the Bay Heist, the Harbor Lights race, and The Getaway — with three named mission givers (Mara Voss, Dario Kess, Officer Reyes). Between jobs there are taxi fares to run and sun coins to collect on Solara Beach, and earning cash unlocks a sports car at the Harbor Garage and the beach cabana.

What's new in v2: four drivable vehicle types with different handling (sedan, sports car, truck, motorcycle), a second district (the Harbor District, with taller towers, a lighthouse and a pier), a bigger high-contrast HUD with a larger minimap (player arrow, police blips, mission marker, north indicator), mission progress and a mission-complete screen, a pause menu, smoother follow camera, real-time shadows, drifting clouds, smarter traffic (cars brake for the car ahead), pedestrians who flee, police who ram toward where you're heading, a wanted level that only cools off while you're hidden, a procedural music loop, engine hum and a car horn, and varied blocky characters with different heights, outfits, hair and hats. All city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound are original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```

Controls: WASD move/drive · mouse drag to look · click to shoot · E enter a car · F interact · Q punch · Space jump · H horn · R restart · V camera reset · Shift run · P pause · C save · M mute. Staged screenshot scenes are available at `?shot=chase|drive|mission|beach&seed=7`.
